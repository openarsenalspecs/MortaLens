# Contributing to MortaLens

Thank you for your interest in contributing to MortaLens. Contributions help improve the accuracy, transparency, security, and accessibility of AI-powered mortgage intelligence and short sale resolution workflows.

## Overview

MortaLens is an open-source, modular mortgage intelligence and short sale management specification. It is designed to support financial institutions, mortgage professionals, service providers, and property owners through structured financial analysis, hardship verification, loan eligibility evaluation, fraud detection, credit impact assessment, and human-supervised decision-making.

Contributions are welcome from individuals and organizations with relevant experience in mortgage lending, loan servicing, financial analysis, short sale processing, real estate, legal and regulatory compliance, artificial intelligence, data security, and related fields.

## Contribution Guidelines

All contributions should support the project's objectives and maintain its commitment to accuracy, transparency, interoperability, privacy, and responsible AI.

Contributors should:

- Provide clear descriptions of proposed changes and their intended benefits.
- Keep specifications modular, consistent, and independently understandable.
- Preserve compatibility with the overall MortaLens architecture.
- Identify assumptions, limitations, dependencies, and relevant industry requirements.
- Distinguish verified information from estimates, recommendations, and AI-generated findings.
- Avoid presenting AI-generated assessments as binding financial, legal, underwriting, or fraud determinations.
- Preserve human review and authorization for final approval, denial, and fraud determinations.
- Protect borrower privacy and confidential financial, identity, tax, employment, and credit information.
- Include appropriate documentation for new capabilities, workflows, and integrations.

## Areas for Contribution

Contributions may include:

- Core mortgage intelligence and short sale evaluation capabilities.
- Financial document analysis and income or asset verification.
- Hardship assessment and loan eligibility evaluation.
- Property valuation and transaction analysis.
- Fraud anomaly detection and evidence review.
- Credit impact assessment and reporting analysis.
- Mortgage loss mitigation and alternative resolution workflows.
- Regulatory compliance and auditability.
- Borrower communication and document management.
- Security, privacy, identity protection, and data governance.
- Optional plugin integrations and enterprise capabilities.
- Documentation, testing, accessibility, and specification improvements.

## Development and Review Process

Before submitting a contribution:

- Review the existing specification to understand its objectives and architecture.
- Check for existing functionality or documentation addressing the proposed change.
- Describe the problem, proposed solution, and expected impact.
- Identify relevant mortgage, financial, legal, regulatory, or technical considerations.
- Include supporting references where applicable.
- Ensure that proposed changes do not introduce unsupported claims or remove necessary safeguards.

Contributions should be reviewed for consistency, accuracy, security, privacy, regulatory considerations, and compatibility with the overall specification.

Changes involving financial eligibility, underwriting, credit analysis, fraud detection, or regulatory interpretation must clearly identify their assumptions and limitations. AI-generated findings must remain traceable to supporting evidence and subject to appropriate human review.

## AI and Decision-Making Standards

MortaLens supports AI-assisted analysis and workflow automation while preserving human authority over consequential decisions.

Contributions must not:

- Treat an AI-generated fraud indicator as proof of fraud.
- Represent preliminary eligibility assessments as guaranteed loan approval.
- Present estimated credit outcomes as guaranteed results.
- Allow automated systems to make final approval or denial decisions without required human authorization.
- Conceal uncertainty, conflicting evidence, or limitations in source information.

Where applicable, contributions should support explainability, evidence traceability, reproducibility, audit records, and documented reviewer overrides.

## Security and Privacy

Contributors must not submit real borrower records, account credentials, Social Security numbers, tax documents, credit reports, or other personally identifiable or confidential financial information.

Security-related contributions should consider data minimization, encryption, access controls, authentication, authorization, retention policies, secure integrations, and protection against unauthorized disclosure.

Potential vulnerabilities or exposures of sensitive information should be reported responsibly rather than publicly disclosed with exploitable details.

## Documentation Standards

Documentation should be clear, accurate, and consistent with the MortaLens specification.

New capabilities should identify their purpose, expected inputs and outputs, applicable workflows, dependencies, limitations, and relevant safeguards.

Changes to existing requirements should explain what is being changed and why. Contributors should preserve established terminology and distinguish mandatory requirements from optional capabilities.

## Community Participation

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Discussions should focus on improving the specification and supporting its intended users.

Suggestions, questions, corrections, and proposals for new modules or integrations are welcome.
